# Privacy policy — Hublot

*Last updated: October 21, 2026*

Hublot is a Windows utility that shows small popups for wireless devices, the music playing, USB drives,
displays and battery. It is published by DeepWaterStudio.

**Hublot does not collect, store or share any personal data.** It has no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no telemetry.

- Everything Hublot reads (device names and battery levels, the title, artist and album art of what is
  playing, USB drive names and free space, display names, battery level) stays on your computer and is
  only used to draw the popups.
- Hublot keeps its settings and the list of devices and media apps it has seen in
  `%LocalAppData%\Hublot` on your computer. They never leave it. Uninstalling Hublot deletes them,
  unless you choose to keep them.
- The Microsoft Store version makes no network connection: updates come from the Store. The version
  downloaded from GitHub checks this repository's releases once a day for a new version (this can be
  turned off in *Settings › About*); this request only contains what any web browser sends to GitHub.
- An optional diagnostic log (off by default) writes what Hublot does to `log.txt` in the same folder;
  it is never sent anywhere.

Questions: open an issue on [github.com/DeepWaters82/Hublot](https://github.com/DeepWaters82/Hublot/issues).

## Politique de confidentialité (français)

**Hublot ne collecte, ne conserve et ne partage aucune donnée personnelle** : ni compte, ni statistiques,
ni publicité, ni télémétrie. Ce qu’il lit (appareils, lecture en cours, clés USB, écrans, batterie) reste
sur votre ordinateur et ne sert qu’à afficher les popups. Ses réglages sont rangés dans
`%LocalAppData%\Hublot` et ne quittent jamais le PC. La version du Microsoft Store ne se connecte pas à
Internet ; celle de GitHub vérifie une fois par jour s’il existe une nouvelle version (désactivable).
